Welcome to my research notes. I'm keeping this space for the short,
in-between ideas — the observations from the lab and the reading that
aren't a full paper, but that I'd like to think through in writing and
come back to later.

## What I'll write about here

Most of what I do sits around **non-volatile memory** and device
reliability: how endurance, retention, and the memory window behave as
devices are scaled and as new material systems come in. Expect notes on:

- small experimental findings and what surprised me
- papers I'm reading and how they change my thinking
- open questions I don't have answers to yet

## Why a notebook, not just a paper

A lot of useful thinking never makes it into a publication. Writing it
down in public keeps me honest, makes the ideas easier to revisit, and
occasionally turns a loose observation into something worth pursuing
properly.

> If an idea is worth having, it's worth writing down badly first.
