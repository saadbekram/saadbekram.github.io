Research Interests

Semiconductor devices & thin-film and functional materials

My interests are in semiconductor device physics and materials characterisation, particularly how defects, strain, and processing conditions determine electrical and optical behaviour. Most of my hands-on work so far is on organic thin-film transistors : fabricating devices and characterising how transfer behaviour, mobility, and threshold voltage change with materials and processing, alongside optical and surface characterisation by UV-Vis and AFM. I am keen to carry this experimental approach into epitaxial semiconductor heterostructures, where the interplay between growth conditions, defects, and carrier dynamics sets device performance.

Research & Project Experience

Master Thesis Researcher

CFAED — Chair of Organic Devices, TU Dresden
Apr 2026 – Present
Master's thesis on OFET-based non-volatile memory
  • Fabricate thin-film memory devices by thermal evaporation (PVD) and nitrogen glove-box processing: a conjugated polymer film serves as the charge-trapping layer, with the semiconducting layer thermally evaporated on top; the device is programmed optically and erased electrically.
  • Investigate how UV-induced crosslinking of the trapping layer affects device behaviour and memory performance.
  • Characterise devices electrically (transfer curves for memory window and field-effect mobility; endurance and retention testing) and by AFM and profilometry; analyse data in Python.

UV Treatment of Polymer Layers in Oxygen-Free Environments

CFAED — Chair of Organic Devices, TU Dresden
2026
