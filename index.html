Research Interests

Semiconductor memory & device physics of non-volatile memory

My research is in semiconductor memory devices and the device physics of non-volatile memory — particularly how reliability behaviour such as endurance, retention, and the memory window depends on device structure, materials, and processing. I am especially interested in how the reliability of a memory device holds up as it is scaled and as new material systems are introduced, and I am keen to extend this toward two-dimensional semiconductors such as transition metal dichalcogenides, where nanofabrication and device physics must be developed together, and toward the memory and logic devices that could be built from them.
